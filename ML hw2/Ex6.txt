In the original space, the model gave a very high error  because it tried to fit a flat surface to non-linear data, ignoring the x1*x2 term.

In the transformed space, we added x1*x2 as a new third feature. Because of this, the model successfully found the correct parameters (3, 2, 1, 1) and the MSE dropped.
This small remaining error is perfectly normal, as it exactly matches the variance of the random noise that we intentionally added at the beginning.
